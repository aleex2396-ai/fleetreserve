# Prior looks

Every look at any data used here before the tag `frozen-plan`, recorded before WP1. Later looks are added with their commit.

## Summary

Held-out data is kept for the final run and was not read row by row before it, except by the full scan of item 53 (in memory, outside the gate, counts only). Training data fits the forecast models; development data is used to choose settings; context sources hold no charging data. A work package (WP) is a numbered build step; a unit is a site or group of sites; the dial is the driver-risk setting; kappa is the forecast caution level; the gate is WP6's go or no-go test; tiers A and B are the driver-protected and deadline-contract readings. Held-out files were downloaded unparsed and hashed as bytes; the Norway split counted held-out rows by site (12,184) without printing a row; the Korean authors' scripts' aggregates were seen; no held-out row was printed; the Norway split program parsed the combined Norway file to separate the sites. After the tag, two looks at held-out files were made outside the gate (items 52 and 53): a layout look at two files, on the owner's yes, and a scan of every row of all three files in memory, for which the decision records the owner's question and no separate yes of the owner's to the scan; the scan printed counts of rows per removal reason (30 Norwegian, 1 a.s.r. and 15,135 Korean rows), the distinct Korean charger-type codes and the earliest and latest Korean days, and no energy, time of day or duration. While the held-out run was going (13:02:48 UTC on 23 Nov to 06:03:31 UTC on 24 Nov), stored outputs of its finished steps were read (item 54): the first read, at 17:37 on 23 Nov, came before the owner's yes of 17:41 (decision 116), the others after it, up to the regeneration of the results document at 05:49 on 24 Nov with 27 of 30 steps ended (the version sent at 05:09 holds a read of a per-day file of a held-out step); five re-runs of the interval step on stored outputs were also made during the run (item 55). After the run ended, further reads of stored outputs and records were made, and the result slots of a paper draft were filled in a copy from WP16's tables (item 56). Seven trials on development data were then announced (item 57) and run (item 58), each made after held-out results were seen and none a result of the plan; an eighth trial, of more cautious bounds on a car's energy, was announced in item 59 and run (item 60): no measurable difference at the one setting tried, and a look at its counts followed (item 61); a second energy-bounds trial, with five arms and one judged comparison, was announced in item 62 and run (item 63): no measurable difference at the one setting tried. The run log, the results document and the owner's decisions named here are working records kept outside the repository.
